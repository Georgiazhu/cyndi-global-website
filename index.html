About Us

关于 Cyndi Global

Cyndi Global Limited 是一家于2025年6月6日在香港注册成立的有限责任公司，办公地址位于香港皇后大道中333-339号建新商业大厦13楼1307室。

我们的核心业务涵盖跨境电商、进出口贸易、国际批发、品牌管理及权益投资。植根于香港，凭借其无可比拟的全球贸易优势，我们为全球合作伙伴提供标准化、完全合规、透明的企业采购以及户外品牌产品的定制加工服务。

受我们温暖、治愈且充满活力的吉祥物 Cyndi 的启发，我们融合中央圣马丁的国际设计视野，为全球领先企业创造独特的品牌体验。

01

Ordering

Step 1

Learn about your corporate event background, communicate details such as product quantity, color, style, material. We provide a product selection list and quotation.

Step 2

Pick specific products from our list, or request more options until you find satisfactory ones.

Step 3

Submit a minimum of 3 design drafts per product, including logos and elements, revise until you confirm the ideal design.

Step 4

Finalize all production specifications and issue the official quotation. Price difference between final and initial quote shall not exceed 10%.

02

Delivery

Step 1

Confirm production lead time and guarantee delivery 7 days ahead of your deadline, leaving ample time for quality inspection.

Step 2

Track full production; submit progress updates and photos every 3 days upon request.

Step 3

Send samples for confirmation; stock remaining goods with warehouse photos. 24hr live camera access available.

Step 4

Deliver goods to designated address, covering full customs clearance and cross-border logistics.

03

Warehousing

We provide warehousing management for all customized products. We maintain detailed inventory records and conduct monthly stocktaking to guarantee accurate inventory quantities.

Initial Warehousing

Count finished goods and create detailed inventory list upon first storage. 24-hour surveillance camera available as optional extra.

Goods Dispatch

Submit transfer requests based on event requirements. Our warehouse team ships the exact quantity to the designated address on schedule.

Re-storage After Events

Unused merchandise can be sent back after quantity checking. We verify the quantity before restocking.

Ongoing Management

Real-time records of all inventory movements. Monthly stock report delivered on the final working day of every month.
